About

Engineer by Training,
Builder by Choice.

My path to engineering started on the factory floor. Managing daily operations for a massive bakery supply chain, I saw firsthand where processes bottlenecked — and instead of waiting for workarounds, I learned to build the exact tools we needed.

My core strength isn't just writing code — it's understanding the operational reality behind the screen. I design the workflow, write the software, and deploy systems that operators actually use every day.

Four years inside a place with no software engineering team, no data infrastructure, and no budget for either. What I had was deep operational context: shelf-life constraints, return rate dynamics, how a network of 6,500+ stores actually behaves day to day.
